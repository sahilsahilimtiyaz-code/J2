Octa-Jarvis is a voice-first assistant that runs on your own device. It thinks offline, speaks in your chosen voice, controls the phone by touch and keeps what it learns about you.

Updates install over the top of your current app. Your API keys, memories and settings are kept.

01

Offline intelligence

Download a GGUF model once, or use the built-in on-device engine. Conversations, memory recall and every phone action keep working with the network switched off.

02

Precise touch control

Jarvis taps by label or pixel, verifies that the screen actually changed, then corrects its aim and remembers the offset for that app.

03

Your voice

Fish Audio, ElevenLabs or the system voice. Barge in at any time and he stops mid-sentence.

04

Update notices

When a new build lands here, the app on your phone offers it on next launch — no store, no account, no tracking.

Install in four taps

  1. 1 Tap Download APK and wait for it to finish in the browser.
  2. 2 Open the downloaded file and allow Install unknown apps for your browser when asked.
  3. 3 Open Octa-Jarvis, finish onboarding, then in App info → three-dot menu choose Allow restricted settings so screen control can be enabled.
  4. 4 Grant microphone, notification and accessibility permissions from the in-app setup wizard.

Installing a newer build over an older one keeps every memory, key and setting. Never uninstall first unless you have exported a backup from Settings.
